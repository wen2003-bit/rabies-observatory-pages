READING THE EVIDENCE

Methods & Interpretation

Study scope

The national evidence layer covers 179 countries and three domains, yielding 537 country-domain assessments.

Evidence cutoff

Official evidence eligible through 4 September 2026 was considered for this study snapshot.

Domain definitions

Human surveillance: national rabies notification, surveillance reporting, exposure or PEP registers, or surveillance-linked laboratory evidence.

Animal surveillance: national rabies notification, investigation, observation, testing, surveillance outputs, or a rabies-specific veterinary programme.

Cross-sector linkage: documented operational connection between human and animal rabies information. Confirmation in both sectors alone does not establish linkage.

Official-source eligibility

Evidence had to come from an identifiable national official authority, apply at national scope, fall within the eligible period, and provide retrievable content that directly met a domain criterion.

Interpreting evidence status

Confirmed means an eligible official source directly satisfied the prespecified criterion.

Not confirmed under protocol means no qualifying national-level official evidence was identified under the prespecified protocol by the evidence cutoff. It does not establish absence of national surveillance.

ABOUT THIS RESOURCE

About

The Rabies Surveillance Evidence Observatory is an interactive companion to a global study of national rabies surveillance architecture and international reporting continuity. It provides country-level access to the official evidence used for the national surveillance evidence layer of the study.

The current version reflects evidence eligible through 4 September 2026 and is not a live surveillance system.

This companion resource accompanies the current study manuscript. A publication citation will be added after formal publication.
